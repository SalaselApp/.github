# Salasel App

<div align="left">

**Salasel App** is a web app that offers carefully curated YouTube playlists for personal development, featuring personal notes linked to the timestamps of each clip within the video, and a system for tracking progress through the playlists and their videos

</div>

## What does Salasel offer?

<div align="left">

**Salasel app** is based on a deep understanding of the needs of today’s youth and offers content that speaks to the whole person through four core pillars:

1. **Sound Nature**
2. **Islamic Faith**
3. **Human Soul**
4. **Physical Health**

> These series are not just random content; they are a journey toward becoming a well-rounded person
